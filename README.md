# nope.

> A powerful screen time management app for iOS that helps you reduce device usage and build healthier digital habits.
                                                                                                           
## Overview                                                                                              

nope. empowers you to take control of your screen time by selectively blocking apps and app categories. With customizable time limits, temporary unlock breaks, and detailed usage statistics, you can monitor your device habits and create meaningful change.

## Key Features

- **Selective App Blocking** – Block individual apps or entire categories
- **Smart Unlocks** – Temporarily unblock apps for a set duration, with automatic re-blocking
- **Usage Statistics** – Track unlock patterns and screen time across days, weeks, months, and years
- **Family Controls Integration** – Leverages Apple's Family Controls framework for secure, system-level blocking

## How It Works

1. **Select apps to block** – Choose which apps or categories you want to limit
2. **Set a schedule** – Activate all-day blocking to restrict access
3. **Take breaks wisely** – Unlock apps temporarily for focused breaks
4. **Monitor progress** – Review detailed statistics to understand your usage patterns
