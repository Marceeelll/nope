# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 25, 2026**

## Overview

nope. ("the App") is built with a simple principle: your data stays on your device. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party.

## Data Collection

**We collect no data.**

The App does not:
- Collect or transmit personal information
- Use analytics or tracking tools
- Connect to any external servers
- Share data with third parties
- Use advertising SDKs

## Apple Frameworks

The App uses Apple's **Family Controls** and **Screen Time** frameworks to block apps. These frameworks are provided by Apple and operate entirely on your device. No usage data is sent to Apple or the developer through these frameworks beyond what Apple's standard iOS data practices cover.

## In-App Purchases

The App offers optional tip jar purchases processed through Apple's **StoreKit** framework. All payment processing is handled by Apple. The developer receives no financial or personal information beyond what Apple provides through standard App Store reporting (aggregate sales data only).

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a revised date.
